## Summary

Jon Parkinson is a product designer based in Margate. He turns complex products into experiences people can understand and systems teams can build on. Most recently he spent five years at Meta, where he led design across integrity, regulatory compliance and account security. Earlier, he owned the final stages of Treatwell’s booking journey, led design at Beautystack and redesigned Pelican Trading with its founders.

His work ranges from designing patterns for millions of people in high-stress situations to drawing individual booking icons and building an iOS app for finding seafood shacks. He started out as an editorial illustrator and still likes making things by hand, from furniture to illustrations.

## What he does

### Design and research

Jon works from discovery through launch. He maps existing journeys and edge cases, tests assumptions with research and data, and uses prototypes to make choices tangible. At Treatwell, he used what people pictured when they heard a category name, then checked those assumptions against what customers actually booked. He used both signals to decide what each icon needed to communicate at small sizes.

### Design leadership

Jon has led projects as an individual contributor. At Meta, he clarified ownership across teams, ran workshops and pre-mortems, and brought engineering, research, product, legal and policy into decisions that affected their work. He made shared patterns easier to adopt with prototypes, documentation and clear rules for when teams could adapt them. He paired with engineers and reviewed builds to catch problems between the design file and what people would actually use.

### Build and AI

Jon uses AI to challenge a brief, explore directions, synthesise material and get to a working prototype sooner. He defines the requirements and interaction rules early, then uses prototypes, dogfooding and implementation reviews to test whether the result holds up. On cross-functional teams that means working alongside engineers. On personal projects he can take a product from brief through build himself.

Shuck It is the clearest example of both uses of AI. Jon used AI tools during the design and build, and built an AI-assisted research workflow that finds potential listings, checks them against inclusion rules and drafts short descriptions in the app’s editorial voice. His earlier web prototype helped establish the interaction model before the iOS beta.

### What he values

Start with user value. Be ruthless about what matters. Give people enough context to make decisions without waiting for permission. Hold a high bar for craft and check whether the work makes a meaningful difference to users and the business. Change direction when the evidence changes.

## What he’s looking for

Jon is interested in senior and staff-level product design roles where he can shape direction, lead work across disciplines and stay hands-on with the product. He’s drawn to design-led teams that care about the details and build things people use in everyday life. He especially likes companion products that help people do something in the real world, like cook, explore or get out of the house. He’s based in Margate and open to UK remote and London hybrid roles. Check current availability with Jon directly.
